Life-days, made tangible

See what’s at stake.
Then win it back.

Every answer is translated into a motivational estimate in Life-days. Three numbers tell the story.

−

Potential loss

What an answer puts at stake in this model. For example, a usual systolic blood pressure of 160 mmHg or above is worth 450 Life-days at stake.

↗

Reclaim up to

How much of that you could realistically win back by taking action. For that blood-pressure answer, up to 125.1 Life-days after weighting and an adherence factor of 0.50.

✦

Claimed

What you have earned by sticking with the habit. Each action carries its own share of the question’s Life-days, and you claim it when you reach the goal.

Life-days are a model-based, motivational estimate. They are not a diagnosis, a measurement, or a prediction of how long you will live. Totals are capped at 9,125 Life-days (25 years). See the Science and Model Registry pages for the method and its limits.

Important: YouthCraft is a wellness and engagement tool, not a diagnostic medical device. Its Life-days are model-derived motivational indicators, not diagnoses or literal predictions of lifespan. They do not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or emergency care.
Quick insights

What the numbers actually mean.

Two examples that show why small, consistent actions matter.

🏃

What 150 minutes of exercise is worth

In the YouthCraft model, moving from under 30 minutes to 150+ minutes of moderate/vigorous activity per week can unlock hundreds of reclaimable Life-days. The Quick Screen asks this first because the upside is large and the action is clear.

📋

The 12 questions that give ~80% of the insight

Phase 0 (Quick Screen) focuses on the highest-impact, easiest-to-answer items: exercise, smoking, alcohol, sleep, BMI/waist, breathlessness, loneliness and a few key preventive checks. Most people get a useful directional estimate from these alone.
