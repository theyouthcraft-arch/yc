The YouthCraft journey

Discover. Craft. Play&Share.

A simple loop that supports reflection, action and continuity—while keeping participation and sharing under your control.

Discover

Complete a structured health profile and receive a starting Youth Score, domain view and lifestyle-informed biological-age perspective.

Craft

Choose a personalised mission connected to an opportunity area such as sleep consistency, movement, nutrition or prevention.

Play

Log or sync habits, follow LIFE DAYS, build consistency streaks and review progress against a weekly goal.

Share

Optionally share a selected score card or take part in a private, time-limited challenge. Nothing is shared publicly by default.

A science-based framework

Designed to connect evidence with everyday life.

The concept paper describes a scoring framework informed by established instruments and guideline-aligned measures. The goal is transparency: make the basis of questions and score contributions traceable, rather than hiding them behind a mystery number.

⌁

Evidence-linked domains

Examples of scientific anchors in the framework include sleep-quality instruments, physical-activity guidance, mood and stress screens, alcohol and tobacco screening tools, clinical marker bands, frailty measures and diet-quality approaches.

  • Domain scores help show where a pattern may need attention.
  • Option-level lookup logic is intended to make score contributions auditable.
  • Age-aware question paths keep the experience relevant across life stages.
◎

A biological-age view—with clear boundaries

YouthCraft proposes a lifestyle-informed biological-age proxy based on domain year-deltas associated with health patterns. It is not presented as a laboratory epigenetic clock, medical test or clinical diagnosis.

  • Youth Score: a composite wellness index out of 100.
  • Domain scores: a more detailed view of contributing areas.
  • LIFE DAYS: a motivational translation of modelled daily gains and losses.

Support that respects your choices

Helpful by design. Yours by choice.

YouthCraft is envisioned as a companion, not a judge. The experience is built around gentle motivation, privacy-conscious sharing and optional pathways to relevant support when patterns suggest that more help may be useful.

♡

Partner, not judge

Imperfect days are expected. The emphasis is on consistency, learning and getting back on track—not shame or perfection.

⌂

Support when relevant

Conceptual pathways may include sleep support, cessation coaching, falls prevention, nutrition programmes or telehealth—only when the user chooses to proceed.

♧

Privacy and control

The operating principles include data minimisation, explicit permissions, user-controlled sharing and the ability to revoke connected data access.

Important: YouthCraft is a wellness and engagement concept, not a diagnostic medical device. Its scores, biological-age view and LIFE DAYS are model-derived motivational indicators—not diagnoses or literal predictions of lifespan. They do not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or emergency care.
