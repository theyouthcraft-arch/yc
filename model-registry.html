1. Overview & Architectural Principles

YouthCraft operates on an open-model framework designed to bridge clinical evidence with daily behavioral tracking. All score outputs are deterministic calculations derived from user-reported questionnaires (up to 107 baseline inputs) and optional daily telemetry (up to 77 recurring metrics).

  • Traceability: Every question option maps to an explicit numeric weight or year-delta range anchored in peer-reviewed literature.
  • Non-Black-Box Model: We reject uninterpretable machine learning predictions in favor of explainable scoring trees and validated clinical screeners.
  • Dynamic Normalization: Scores adjust according to age-specific and gender-specific baseline cohorts.

2. Core Scoring Modules

Youth Score (0–100 Index)

A weighted composite index aggregating six health domains. Normalization factors prevent individual outliers from distorting overall wellness trends.

Domain Year-Deltas (Δ-Years)

Models biological age deviations (-5.0 to +5.0 years) per domain based on epidemiological hazard ratios and lifestyle impact studies.

LIFE DAYS Metric

A motivational translation converting sustained positive daily choices into estimated fractional quality-adjusted days gained.
