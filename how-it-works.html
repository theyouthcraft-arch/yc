A simple, repeatable loop

Your journey in seven steps

Health change can feel complicated. YouthCraft breaks the experience into a sequence that is easy to understand and return to—without asking you to become your own clinician.

01

Measure

Build your baseline with an age-aware assessment of up to 107 questions, with optional wearable or health-data connections.

02

Discover

See your Youth Score, lifestyle-age view, domain patterns and a meaningful opportunity to focus on first.

03

Improve

Receive one small, achievable daily mission matched to an area where a change may be useful.

04

Score

Track mission completion, streaks, Health Points and changes in your LIFE DAYS bank.

05

Share

Create a result card highlighting the progress you choose to share with your circle or social channels.

06

Challenge

Invite a friend, spouse, family member or colleague to join an optional, friendly weekly challenge.

07

Repeat

Return tomorrow, keep building consistency, and review how your patterns change over time.

Trust is part of the journey

Science-informed. Privacy-conscious. Always your choice.

YouthCraft's concept puts transparency, user control and clear wellness boundaries at the centre of the experience.

🔬

Science-first scoring

Scoring rules are intended to map to published instruments and established guidelines, including PSQI, IPAQ, PHQ-2 and AUDIT-C where relevant.

🛡️

Clear wellness boundary

App scores, measured clinical values and medical findings are distinct. YouthCraft is not a diagnostic medical device and does not replace professional care.

🙌

Optional partner pathways

When patterns meet defined thresholds, relevant support options may be presented. You remain in control of whether to proceed or share information.

🔐

Privacy and consent

The design calls for data minimisation, explicit permissions and user-controlled sharing, with safeguards against workplace or third-party surveillance.
