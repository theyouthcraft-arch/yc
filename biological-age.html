Understanding Biological Age in YouthCraft

Learn how lifestyle patterns translate into a biological age perspective—and how small daily choices shift your trajectory over time.

1. What is Biological Age?

While your chronological age counts the exact number of years since birth, your biological age reflects the functional state of your body and tissues. Two individuals at age 45 may have vastly different risk profiles and physiological vitality depending on genetics, environment, and daily habits.

2. The YouthCraft Proxy vs. Epigenetic Clocks

It is important to understand what YouthCraft's biological age metric is—and what it is not:

YouthCraft Biological Age Proxy

  • Method: Non-invasive behavioral and physiological questionnaire + daily habits telemetry.
  • Purpose: Motivational feedback loop to identify actionable lifestyle leverage points.
  • Cost & Speed: Instant, zero-cost, continuous real-time recalculation.

Clinical Epigenetic / DNA Clocks

  • Method: DNA methylation sequencing (e.g., Horvath/GrimAge) or blood panel analysis.
  • Purpose: Clinical research and deep molecular biological age estimation.
  • Cost & Speed: Requires blood/saliva sampling, lab analysis, and higher cost.

3. How Domain Deltas Accumulate

Your YouthCraft Biological Age is calculated using your chronological age as an anchor, modified by your Domain Year-Deltas (Δ):

Biological Age = Chronological Age + ∑ (Domain Deltas)

For example, a 40-year-old with optimal sleep (-1.2 yrs) and regular movement (-1.5 yrs), but poor stress management (+0.8 yrs) and missing preventive screens (+0.4 yrs) would receive a net delta of -1.5 years, yielding a Biological Age Proxy of 38.5 years.

4. Turning Insights into Action: LIFE DAYS

Biological age doesn't change overnight. To make long-term longevity actionable, YouthCraft translates positive daily choices into LIFE DAYS—a tangible unit representing quality time added back to your future self through continuous habit consistency.
