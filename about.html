01 · Who we are

Make the long game of health feel personal.

YouthCraft is for people who want to stay healthier and more active for longer, not through extreme measures or medical fatigue, but through small, measurable habits that build over time. We turn complex health information into clear signals, practical next steps and a daily experience people can return to.

Lifespan and healthspan are not the same. Living longer does not automatically mean more years feeling well, capable and independent. YouthCraft is built on the idea that prevention should feel approachable, understandable and part of everyday life.

“Small, sustainable choices. A clearer view of your health journey.” That is our guiding idea: turn awareness into action, without judgement.

05 · Ideas we are exploring

Not part of today’s version.

These are directions, not features you can use now.

  • Private, time-limited challenges with friends, family or colleagues.
  • Optional pathways to specialist support (for example sleep support, cessation coaching or telehealth) with clear consent at each step.
  • Yearly summaries of your progress.
  • Accounts and syncing across devices.
Life-days and Life-years are motivational model outputs, not literal days or years added to an individual’s life. Any scoring, weights and thresholds are published on the Model Registry, with their status.
