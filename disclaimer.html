1. No Doctor-Patient Relationship

Use of YouthCraft does not establish a doctor-patient or clinical relationship. Always consult a qualified physician or healthcare professional regarding any medical conditions, biometrics, symptoms, or lifestyle adjustments.

2. Emergency Situations

YouthCraft is not designed or monitored for emergency response. If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency services (e.g., 911, 112) or go to the nearest hospital emergency room immediately.

The Youth Score, biological age proxies, and LIFE DAYS metrics are mathematical model outputs derived from population averages and lifestyle correlation research.
