Missions: check in, get feedback

Each question you committed to is broken into clear, trackable steps with a direct question, for example “How many alcoholic drinks did you have today?” with answers None, 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5+. Tap one button and you instantly see Excellent Good or Needs action with a short, practical message.

Some steps only appear when they matter. If you report three or more drinks, a support question appears. Red-flag questions show a clear alert when it is time to speak to a professional. Anything that doesn’t apply to you can be moved out with Not relevant to me.

Streaks count consecutive days, weeks or months without a “Needs action”, so a good rhythm is rewarded, not perfection.

Done & Dusted: claim your Life-days

When an action has become a habit, it graduates. For a daily action that means 21 positive check-ins in the last 30 days. You tap I am over this, Claim my lost lifedays, the action leaves your mission list, and its Life-days are added to your total.

Once-off actions such as a test or a check-up move across by themselves when you mark them done. Actions that don’t apply to you also land here, with no Life-days claimed.

Want to follow up again? Tap Start tracking again. The action returns to your missions, its check-in count starts fresh, and its Life-days go back to “still to claim” until you earn them again.

The numbers behind each card

How a Life-day figure is built.

Here is the worked example for Q001, “What is your usual systolic blood pressure?”, with the answer “160 mmHg or above”.

450Potential loss: the most Life-days this question can put at stake in the model.
270Recoverable with action: 60% of the potential loss can be modified.
250.3Weighted: ×0.927, an effective weight that reduces double counting with related questions and adjusts for age and sex.
125.1Reclaim up to: ×0.50 adherence factor, because few people sustain every action.

That 125.1 is then shared across the question’s action steps (for Q001: salt, exercise and medicines, 34%, 33% and 33%). Each share is what you claim when that step is done. Steps that only measure something, or only appear as a follow-up, carry no share.

Check-in rhythms

When does an action count as done?

Each action step has its own rhythm and its own goal. These are the current defaults.

RhythmStepsGood forReady to claim after
Daily334Habits such as salt, steps, alcohol, sleep21 positive days within the last 30
Weekly196Sessions and routines, like strength training3 positive weeks within the last 4
Monthly64Trends such as weight and symptoms3 positive months within the last 4
Quarterly19Check-ups and repeat tests2 positive quarters within the last 2
Event-based198Tests, visits and warning signsOnce, when it is done (moves automatically)

“Positive” means Excellent or Good for daily, weekly and monthly steps, and Excellent only for quarterly and once-off steps, so “Booked” does not count as done. Of the 811 steps, 250 are tracking-only (measurements, follow-ups and red-flag checks) and carry no Life-days. Only the daily 21-in-30 rule was set by the YouthCraft team; the other thresholds are defaults that may change.

Good to know

Questions people ask.

Do I need an account?

Not in this version. The onboarding, mission and Done & Dusted pages work without signing in. Accounts may come later.

Where is my data stored?

In your own browser, on the device you are using. These pages do not send your answers or check-ins to a server, so they do not sync between devices, and clearing your browser data removes them.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing is taken away. Life-days you have already claimed stay claimed. A missed day simply doesn’t count toward the next claim, and your streak starts again when you do.

Are Life-days real days added to my life?

No. Life-days are a modelled, motivational translation of research on health risks into something easy to follow. They are not a measurement, a diagnosis or a prediction of how long you will live.

Can I connect a watch or upload a lab report?

Not in this version. Check-ins are quick manual taps and entries today.

Is this medical advice?

No. YouthCraft supports reflection and habit-building. Please speak to a qualified professional about your health, and call emergency services in an emergency.
