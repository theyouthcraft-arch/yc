01 / Who we are

Make the long game of health feel personal.

YouthCraft is designed for people who want to stay healthier and more active for longer—not through extreme measures or medical fatigue, but through small, measurable habits that can build over time. We translate complex health and lifestyle information into clear signals, practical next steps and a daily experience people can return to.

Lifespan and healthspan are not the same: living longer does not automatically mean spending more years feeling well, capable and independent. YouthCraft is built around the idea that prevention should feel approachable, understandable and part of everyday life.

❝
“Small, sustainable choices. A clearer view of your health journey.”
YouthCraft's guiding idea: turn awareness into action, without judgement.
02 / Our vision

A world where prevention feels empowering.

We want every person to better understand how daily routines may shape their long-term health—and to have practical ways to improve those routines over time.

🧭

Clear, encouraging and actionable

Rather than intimidating dashboards or clinical lectures, YouthCraft aims to use intuitive language, playful identities, achievable missions and supportive social experiences. The goal is to help people build sustainable wellness habits while keeping the limits of health estimates clear.

03 / What we're here to do

Five goals. One everyday experience.

YouthCraft turns broad preventive-health ambitions into smaller, more approachable actions.

📈
GOAL 01

Visualise long-term impact

Bring sleep, movement, nutrition and daily habits into an understandable Youth Score and biological-age view, with transparent context about what the measures can—and cannot—tell you.

🎯
GOAL 02

Simplify daily action

Reduce overload by turning broad wellness goals into one clear, achievable daily mission at a time.

🔥
GOAL 03

Make consistency rewarding

Use points, streaks, milestones and positive feedback to encourage repeatable habits—not perfection.

🤝
GOAL 04

Connect through community

Support privacy-aware challenges and shared goals with friends, family and colleagues, so encouragement can become part of the journey.

🩺
GOAL 05

Make support easier to find

Where relevant risk factors cluster, the intended model can connect users with specialised health partners—for example, sleep support, cessation coaching or cardiology telehealth—using appropriate safeguards.

🌱
THE APPROACH

Progress over perfection

Build a rhythm that can survive real life. Missed days are expected; the focus is on learning, returning and making the next useful choice.

04 / Motivation that adds up

Make progress visible—with LIFE DAYS.

YouthCraft's proposed gamification experience is designed to turn everyday consistency into feedback users can understand and celebrate.

🌙

Everyday micro-missions

Small actions around sleep consistency, movement, nutrition and stress management.

🌟

Celebrate consistency

Streaks and milestones recognise a reliable rhythm, not flawless performance.

🏅

Personal milestones

Health Personality badges and annual “Health Wrapped” summaries make progress easier to reflect on.

👥

Friendly social loops

Optional challenges and shared achievements can make healthy routines more engaging.

LIFE DAYS and LIFE YEARS are intended as motivational model outputs, not literal days or years added to an individual's life. Any numerical scoring, weights and thresholds must be transparently specified and appropriately validated before being presented as calibrated estimates.
