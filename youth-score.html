Three numbers, one story

How the scorecard works.

−

Potential loss

Shown in onboarding: what an answer puts at stake in the model, in Life-days.

↗

Still to claim

Shown on your missions page: the Life-days you could reclaim from the actions still in your list, with years in brackets.

✦

Claimed

Shown here and on Done & Dusted: Life-days earned by sticking with an action. Life-years gained = Life-days ÷ 365.25.

Read this as motivation, not measurement. Life-days are model-based estimates. They are not a diagnosis, a clinical marker or a prediction of how long you will live. Claims reflect your own entries. If you start tracking an action again, its Life-days return to “still to claim” until you earn them again. See the Model Registry for the parameters.
